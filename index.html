The 4 Actions Explained

🚩Report Suspicious

Flag the message/call as a threat. Best for clear scams. Wrong for safe messages.

🚫Ignore

Delete without acting. Often acceptable. May miss real messages.

🔍Verify Source

Check authenticity independently before acting. Best for ambiguous cases.

✅Proceed Carefully

Trust and act on the message. Right for legitimate messages. Wrong for scams.

🧠 Smart Tips

The Real Rules

🔍

Check Sender Domains

Verify the full email address. "amaz0n.com" ≠ "amazon.com". Look for subtle typos.

⚖️

Not All Messages Are Scams

Real alerts, delivery notifications, and bank messages exist. Use Verify before acting on them.

🔍

Verify Independently

Never use links in the suspect message. Go directly to the official website or call a known number.

⏰

Urgency Is a Tactic

"Act now" and "Account blocked" are pressure tactics. Slow down — legitimate issues can wait for verification.

🔒

Never Share OTPs

No legitimate organization will ever ask for your OTP, PIN, or password via any channel.

⚠️

Over-Reporting Is Harmful

Reporting legitimate messages causes alert fatigue, wasting security team resources. Verify first.
